## Downloaded for NIRS-8

1. `02_Kumar_2023_PHM_rotating_machinery_industrial_robot_review.pdf`
   - Kumar P., Khalid S., Kim H.S. Prognostics and Health Management of Rotating Machinery of Industrial Robot with Deep Learning Applications - A Review. Mathematics, 2023.
   - Use for: PHM of industrial-robot rotating machinery, bearings/gears, deep-learning feature extraction, RUL context.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.3390/math11133008`

2. `03_Xiao_2024_digital_twin_driven_PHM_industrial_assets.pdf`
   - Xiao et al. Digital twin-driven prognostics and health management for industrial assets. Scientific Reports, 2024.
   - Use for: digital twin and PHM framing.
   - Link: `https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-63990-0`

3. `04_Hu_2019_RUL_model_assessment_mechanical_products_review.pdf`
   - Hu Y. et al. Remaining Useful Life Model and Assessment of Mechanical Products: A Brief Review and a Note on the State Space Model Method. Chinese Journal of Mechanical Engineering, 2019.
   - Use for: RUL models for mechanical products, physics-based vs data-driven methods.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.1186/s10033-019-0317-y`

4. `05_Wang_2021_bearing_RUL_deep_spatiotemporal_CNN.pdf`
   - Wang X. et al. Deep Spatiotemporal Convolutional-Neural-Network-Based Remaining Useful Life Estimation of Bearings. Chinese Journal of Mechanical Engineering, 2021.
   - Use for: bearing RUL and time-series feature extraction.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.1186/s10033-021-00576-1`

5. `06_Real_time_AI_driven_PHM_in_robotics_2026.pdf`
   - Tanveer M. et al. Real-Time AI-Driven Prognostics and Health Management in Robotics. Applied Sciences, 2026.
   - Use for: current robotics PHM taxonomy, real-time constraints, sensors, health indicator/RUL definitions.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.3390/app16073441`

6. `07_Wojtulewicz_2025_industrial_robot_control_predictive_maintenance_IIoT.pdf`
   - Wojtulewicz A., Chaber P. Industrial Robot Control System with a Predictive Maintenance Module Using IIoT Technology. Sensors, 2025.
   - Use for: practical industrial robot diagnostics, current/velocity-based consumption indicators, MQTT/IIoT, gear/bearing/belt/lubrication usage.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.3390/s25041154`

7. `08_Predictive_maintenance_fault_detection_motor_drive_industrial_robots_CNN_RNN_2025.pdf`
   - Eang C., Lee S. Predictive Maintenance and Fault Detection for Motor Drive Control Systems in Industrial Robots Using CNN-RNN-Based Observers. Sensors, 2025.
   - Use for: motor-drive fault detection, sensor features, CNN-RNN predictive-maintenance example.
   - DOI/link: `https://doi.org/10.3390/s25010025`
